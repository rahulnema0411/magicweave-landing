### Why Choose Magicweave?

Benefits designed to provide a seamless & secure way for our developers to offer all gamification, retention systems in their titles.

Persistent Player Identity

Players don’t restart with every new game. Profiles, progress, and activity carry across the entire network.

Shared Rewards & Economy

Offer real, redeemable rewards through a unified system — not isolated, disposable in-game currency.

Built-in Monetization

Ads, rewards, and in-app systems are already integrated — optimized to work from day one.

Network-Driven Growth

Your game doesn’t grow alone. It benefits from shared users, rewards, and distribution across the network.
